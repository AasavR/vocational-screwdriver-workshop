Meta Quest Standalone XR Experience

Developed in Unity 6 (6000.6.0f1) targeting Meta Quest 2, Quest 3, and Quest Pro headsets. The module delivers a calibrated vocational training scenario teaching precision screwdriver mechanics, fastener alignment, thread release, and mechanical assembly procedures.

  • Kinematic Isolation: Screwdriver movement is strictly isolated to the tool tip, preventing accidental translation of the workbench or workpiece.
  • Target Proximity Snapping: Automatic magnetic engagement when within 0.12m tolerance radius of countersunk screw heads.
  • Synchronized Unthreading: Real-time rotational velocity (360°/s) coupled with proportional upward lift (0.025m travel) along the screw thread pitch.
  • Tactile Feedback: Haptic pulse train on Oculus Touch controllers synchronized with ratcheting audio clicks.
